# Rockbuster Analytics — Findings & Recommendations

**Data window:** 14 Feb 2007 – 14 May 2007. Feb and May are partial months, so their low totals are an artifact of the window, not a real decline.

## Headline numbers
- **Total revenue:** $61,312.04 from 14,596 payments
- **Customers:** 599 · **Films:** 1,000 · **Rentals:** 16,044
- **Average revenue per customer:** $102.36
- **Films never rented:** 43

## Q1 — Revenue trend
- **Insight:** Revenue grew 19.6% from March ($23,887) to April ($28,559), the two full months in the data.
- **Recommendation:** Keep the March–April acquisition and promotion mix, and collect a longer time window before forecasting seasonality.

## Q2 — Top films per category
- **Insight:** Sports ($4,892), Sci-Fi ($4,336) and Animation ($4,245) are the highest-earning categories; Music ($3,072) and Travel ($3,227) are the lowest. Top titles per category earn roughly $145–$170 each (e.g. *Dogma Family* in Animation, *Fool Mockingbird* in Action).
- **Recommendation:** Stock extra copies of each category's top-3 titles and feature them in promotions; review the Music and Travel catalog.

## Q3 — Customer segments (RFM)
- **Insight:** Regular is the largest segment (243 customers), followed by Big Spenders (152), Champions (147) and At Risk (57).
- **Recommendation:** Reward Champions with a loyalty offer, and send the 57 At Risk customers a win-back campaign before they churn.

## Q4 — Above-average customers
- **Insight:** 285 of 599 customers (48%) spend more than the $102.36 average. The top spender is Eleanor Hunt at $211.55.
- **Recommendation:** Use this group as the base for a loyalty or membership tier.

## Q5 — Dead inventory
- **Insight:** 43 films have never been rented.
- **Recommendation:** Discount or bundle these titles, or remove them to free shelf space and inventory cost.

## Q6 — Revenue by country
- **Insight:** The top 3 markets are India ($6,035), China ($5,251) and the United States ($3,685), together 24.4% of revenue.
- **Recommendation:** Focus marketing spend on these three markets first.

## Q7 — Store & staff performance
- **Insight:** The two stores are almost even: Store 2 (Jon Stephens) handled 50.7% of revenue ($31,060) and Store 1 (Mike Hillyer) 49.3% ($30,252).
- **Recommendation:** Performance is balanced, so no staffing change is needed; track this split monthly to catch any drift.

## Q8 — Rental duration
- **Insight:** 29.2% of rentals are kept 7+ days, the single largest band; 26.3% come back in under 3 days.
- **Recommendation:** Review late-fee rules, or offer a paid "extended rental" option to turn long rentals into revenue.
